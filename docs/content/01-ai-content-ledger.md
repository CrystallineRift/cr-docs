# AI Content Ledger

Every piece of game content that was generated by AI is listed here. **All of it is placeholder and must be replaced (or rewritten and approved by a human) before launch.** Code is not listed; player-facing text, names, lore, level geometry, generated assets and placeholder art are.

**Rule:** the change that adds or edits AI content adds or updates its row here, with dates. A row only moves to *Final* when a human has replaced or approved it.

**Status values:** `AI draft` (generated, untouched) · `User-edited` (a human has revised it, not yet signed off) · `Final` (human-made or human-approved — ready to ship).
