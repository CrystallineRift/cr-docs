# World Fill Content

Side quests, optional dungeons, zones and pickups added by the world-fill program. All text is AI draft (see the [AI Content Ledger](?page=content/01-ai-content-ledger)). The packet schema and lint are in [Side Content Packets](?page=unity/46-side-content-packets); the dungeon spec in [Dungeon Scaffold](?page=unity/47-dungeon-scaffold). Zone and pickup positions were later checked with the nav probe and moved onto reachable ground where needed (`nav-snap-ledger.json`, see [World Navigability](?page=unity/49-world-navigability#overworld-rows-on-reachable-ground)); moved rows need a Studio content push.

Generated by `scripts/gen-world-fill.py` from `Assets/CR/Game/Story/Content/Side/*.json` and `Assets/CR/Game/OpenWorld/Data/Dungeons/*.json`. Do not edit the tables by hand.
